### Phone
- Fixed: taps could take seconds to register, and seemed to wait for the sync with the server to finish. After every edit and sync, the app re-read every activity's full GPS detail and every day's heart-rate history, which kept the phone busy and held up anything you did meanwhile. Both are now read once and kept until a watch or import changes them.
- Race plans re-plan as the intensity and split sliders move: the finish, laps, graph and fuel follow your finger instead of updating seconds after you let go.

### Race plans
- The race intensity slider sits under the split slider (below the map on the phone) instead of in a section of its own.

### Training
- A goal can be marked complete, which takes it out of the active slot. Completed goals are listed on their own, and activating one again reopens it.
- The run on race day now ticks off the race on the plan by itself, as any other day's run ticks off that day's session.
