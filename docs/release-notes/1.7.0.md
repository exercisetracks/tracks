Offline maps have been rebuilt from end to end. The phone now saves an area as a set of map files rather than fetching it tile by tile, so you can keep as much map as your phone has room for — whole states or countries — on its own storage or an SD card. Downloads carry on with the app closed, show their progress in a notification, resume where they stopped after a dropped connection, and are checked before they are used. Saved areas also keep their trails, contours and terrain offline after the server changes; before, a download or delete anywhere on the server could leave them showing only the basemap. Areas saved the old way are moved over automatically on Wi-Fi. You can also build an area on the server only, without saving it to the phone.

On the server, map building is much faster and lighter. Adding a small area took 7.7 minutes in testing and now takes about a minute; removing one takes seconds. Each map is stored once instead of twice, overlapping areas no longer have to be merged, and builds run one at a time and pick up where they left off after a restart. Size estimates come back in a fraction of a second and count only what is new. Progress is a single bar that only moves forward, including the trail-data download. Oceans, seas, gulfs and lakes are now named on the map, and national forests and other public land that reach past a downloaded area are no longer left out — areas you already have rebuild their trail layer once, automatically, to pick this up. Removing an area from the server is now admin-only and asks first.

On the phone, all status — syncing, the watch, backups, map downloads, and results — now appears in one pill at the top that you can tap for details, and pull-to-sync works on every page. If the watch stops sending its activities and health files, Tracks now tells you to restart it, which is what clears that state. Signing out takes effect immediately. Connecting the phone to a different server now combines the phone's data with the server's instead of deleting the server's.

On the web, if your session's encryption key expires, Tracks asks for your password instead of showing empty pages, and contour generation no longer runs out of memory on smaller servers.
