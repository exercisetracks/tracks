### Phone
- The phone no longer crashes on launch after a large history import. Health screens now read your days a page at a time instead of all at once.
- Calls ring on the watch as soon as the phone rings, instead of a couple of seconds later.
- The watch shows the caller's name from your contacts instead of only their number.
- Replying to a call from the watch now texts the caller, with any phone app; before, it could fail with "the phone app did not share the caller's number".
- Settings → Notifications on the watch has one "Calls on the watch" switch, in place of "Text callers your reply". Turn it on to allow the phone, call log, contacts and SMS permissions it needs. Without it, calls still ring, but later and without the caller's name.
- The watch's music controls show the song title, not the current lyric line, with players that send lyrics to Bluetooth.
- Notifications and calls no longer wait behind music updates on their way to the watch.

### Web
- The activity list shows 100 activities per page.
