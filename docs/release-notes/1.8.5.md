### Phone and watch
- The watch's activity in progress now really does update on the phone's map about once a second. The phone now asks the watch for its position the way Garmin Explore does; before, the watch never sent it.
- While the watch records a GPS activity, a "Recording on the watch" banner shows on every page. Tap it to open the map.
- The dashboard's Start activity button is greyed out while the watch is recording.
- The start of a watch's track no longer jumps to the North Pole when the watch began recording before it had GPS.
- Choosing sync on the watch now starts a sync with Tracks.
- Water logged in the watch's hydration widget is added to that day's hydration in Tracks, and the widget shows Tracks' total for today. Your watch's first hydration sync turns this on.
- Pull-to-sync no longer shows "Syncing watch failed: rememberCoroutineScope left the composition" when you leave the page while it is syncing.

### Under the hood
- The watch connection code is updated to Gadgetbridge 0.95.1, which fixes a decoder that could get stuck on a cut-off message, a connection leak, a crash on disconnect, and slow or failing weather messages on watches with a small Bluetooth link.
- Wind speed on the watch is now correct (it was about 7% high).
