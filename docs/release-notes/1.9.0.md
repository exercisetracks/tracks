### Race plans
- One "Add course" button holds every way to get a course: upload a GPX, draw it on the map, or pick a saved track. Once a course is loaded it becomes "Replace course".
- The file picker only offers GPX files.
- The lap table is gone. A graph of the race takes its place on every plan: each lap's target as a step (pace, power, or HR ceiling for mountain biking), the HR ceiling as a dashed line, and the course's hills as a shape behind them. Hover or hold it to read any point and mark it on the map.
- The phone now plans cycling, mountain biking, swimming and triathlon races itself, exactly as the server does. Before, it planned running only.
- Triathlons take a GPX for the whole course, one per leg, or both. A whole course is split at T1 and T2 automatically, and you drag either one on the graph to correct it. Each leg's hills shape its targets, and each leg gets its own graph.
- The "Race-day strategy" card is gone. Its average HR ceiling and hill-climb W/kg target are now one line under the split slider.

### Fuelling
- Four gauges show carbs, sodium, potassium and fluid per hour. Each fills to what your products deliver and has a tick at your target, on a scale that runs to an elite ultra athlete's intake.
- Products and targets now include potassium.
- Your stops are shown on a strip along the race clock, with the full list folded underneath.
- New built-in "Homemade mix": a drink made from household ingredients (Basic, High-carb or Natural recipe), in grams and spoons per bottle, with how many bottles to make. It makes up whatever your other products leave short, and says how much to eat as well when that would make the drink too strong for the day's heat.
- Adding a product offers a list of common race foods with their label nutrition (GU, Honey Stinger, Clif, Maurten, Tailwind, Gatorade, Snickers, Rice Krispies Treats, bananas), or a custom product of your own.
- The lap table's "Sip X ml" column and the older carbs-per-hour target are gone: the fuel plan covers every sport.

### Activities
- Hidden activities are left out of the dashboard, training load, readiness and the heatmap on the server, as on the phone. Choose "Hidden" in the activity list's sport filter to find them; you can hide and show activities on the web as well as on the phone.

### Phone
- The watch-link notification is silent and no longer says "Connecting…" while connected.
- Fitness and volume charts show the year on long time windows.
- The drawer always opens a page at its start.
- The race plan page loses its top back button (use the system back) and the "Garmin workout targets" label.
