### Maps
- Icons on the web map are drawn with the right symbols again. After the icon sheet was last updated, a browser could keep part of the old sheet, and every icon shifted one place: parks showed the public-works symbol, peaks the park's, some trails the hospital's. The sheet is now named by its contents, so a browser can only ever keep a matching copy. Reload the map page once after updating.

### Health
- The SpO2 dial shows your latest reading, matching the watch, instead of the day's average, which overnight readings pulled down. Tapping it opens a chart of every reading.
- The SpO2 history chart is one joined line, with dots only at its ends, and breaks across gaps of more than two days.
- Intensity minutes count the calendar week from Monday, as the watch does, instead of the last seven days.

### Activities
- Activity notes can be written and edited on the web and the phone, from the pencil beside the activity's name.
- On the phone, the activity map has the web's colour pills (heart rate, speed, cadence, gradient, and grit and flow on mountain bike rides) with a legend, and follows the app's light or dark theme.
- On the phone, rename an activity by tapping its title. Correcting an activity's sport is no longer offered there.
- On the web, the route map's legend no longer draws over dialogs.

### Dashboard
- The activity heatmap has the map page's toolbar (compass, 3D, zoom) and its wheel zoom, so tilting it no longer needs a touchpad gesture. It follows light and dark mode and is taller.

### Phone
- The status pill goes away on time during a long sync, instead of staying up until the sync finished. Swipe it sideways or up to dismiss it.
