### Watch sync
- Courses and places no longer fail to sync now and then while the phone shows an out-of-date watch battery level. When the watch quietly restarts its side of the connection, the phone now notices and reconnects by itself; until now it took force-stopping the app.
- A failed course and place sync reconnects to the watch and tries again before telling you.
- The watch's battery level is no longer shown while the watch is disconnected.
- Garmin watches without Garmin Explore support now get courses and saved places as files, instead of not at all.
- Settings → Watch has an Unpair button while a watch is paired, in place of the "I have a Garmin watch" switch.

### Health
- The Health page loads much faster: it fetches only the dates on screen, and the server keeps the daily summaries ready.
- Heart-rate and stress history charts show the time, value and zone under your pointer, like the other charts.
- Charts covering more than about half a year show the year on their dates, on the web and the phone.
- The sleep score is a smooth line without per-night dots, and on long windows it is thinner and fainter so the sleep stages underneath stay readable.

### Web
- Settings can import a whole Garmin export at once: pick its folder, drop it, or choose its .zip files. An import that stops picks up where it left off, and keeps running if you leave the page.
- Settings can export every original FIT file as one zip, sorted by kind.
