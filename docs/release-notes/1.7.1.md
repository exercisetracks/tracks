### Phone and watch
- Incoming phone calls now ring on the watch until they are answered, declined or end, and can be answered or declined from the wrist.
- Replying to a call from the watch declines it and texts the caller your chosen quick reply. Turn this on under Settings › Notifications on the watch › "Text callers your reply" (it asks for permission to send texts).

### Signed-in devices
- A phone no longer appears again in the web's device list every time it updates or reconnects; existing duplicates clear the next time each phone signs in.
- Signing out on the phone now always ends its session on the server, even after time offline.
- Each phone in Settings › Version on the web has a Sign out button, which signs it out of the server and removes it from the list.
