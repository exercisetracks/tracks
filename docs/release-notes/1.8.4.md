### Phone
- The watch's activity in progress now updates on the phone's map about once a second while the watch records, instead of every twenty minutes or so. The watch's position reports were being dropped on arrival.
- While the watch records, the location dot on the map is the watch's position, shown with a Bluetooth symbol. Its arrow still follows the phone's compass. The dot turns grey if the watch goes quiet, and goes back to the phone's own position after two minutes.
- The watch's track on the map is drawn in your theme's accent colour.
- A run or ride recorded with the phone's GPS now draws its track on the map as you go, in the accent colour.
- The workout banner, which leads back to the workout in progress, now also shows on the map.
