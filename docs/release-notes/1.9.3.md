### Running paces
- Easy and threshold paces follow Daniels' own tables: easy runs sit in the middle of the easy range instead of at its fast edge (about 20–30 s/km slower), threshold about 5 s/km slower. Plans rebuild with the new paces on their own.
- A watch's VO₂max estimate is no longer read as race fitness one for one, and treadmill and virtual runs no longer set it, since their speed is an estimate.
- Running with the phone alone, without a heart-rate strap, fitness now comes from the last eight weeks of distance and pace rather than from the fastest efforts only.

### Race plans
- The prediction is for race day: today's fitness plus the plan's progress still to come, scaled by how much of the plan the last four weeks actually delivered. Missed sessions lower it, extra training raises it, and by race day it is simply your fitness. Today's finish is shown beside it.
- Other sports count toward a running plan at what they transfer: a ride about half, a swim about a third, climbing barely. A session ticked off by hand counts half.
- Race morning's freshness after the taper is used, rather than how tired you are today.
- Hills count consistently: every climb from 200 m long gets its own target, shorter hills slow the split they are in, rolling roads cost time, and the finish no longer depends on how the course is split up.
- Climbs are priced from measurements of runners rather than walkers: the target up a long steady climb is a little faster than before, while short climbs and rolling roads, which were mostly ignored, now slow their targets and the finish.
- The goal card's predicted time includes the course's hills.
- Triathlons: the run leg's time includes its hills, and the terrain type applies to legs without a course.
- The intensity slider goes to 20 % easier or harder than predicted (was 10 %).
- The phone and the web now give the same prediction, apart from the weather forecast only the web has.

### Watch
- The race is on the watch as the training calendar's race-day workout, with the plan's splits, targets and fuelling reminders. Before, it went as an empty workout unless the race plan page had been opened first.
- The race's course goes to the watch's courses for the days the race is on the calendar, and comes off afterwards. On race day, start it from the navigation menu to see it on the map.
- Fixed: on a server with more than one account, one account's race file could be sent to another account's watch.
- A race file sent to the watch the old way is removed by the next cable or browser sync.

### Phone
- On race day, the race plan offers Start race: the guided run with the plan's splits and spoken cues.
