### Training without a watch
- Runs, rides, walks and timed sessions recorded with the phone now count toward fitness, fatigue, form, readiness and VO₂max. Until now each one was read as lasting a few seconds, so for anyone recording with the phone alone those charts stayed flat at their starting values however much they trained.
- Activities already recorded on the phone get their real length back automatically — on the server at your next sign-in, on the phone the next time the app opens. Planned workouts they matched are ticked off if they now count as done.
- Fitness reads right from the start of a history. Someone who has just started recording no longer shows as having almost no fitness for the first months: three weeks of steady training now read as that training. Long histories are unchanged.

### Race plans
- Race plans have an intensity slider: race up to a tenth easier or harder than predicted — pace on foot and in the water, power on a bike — with every target and the finish following.
